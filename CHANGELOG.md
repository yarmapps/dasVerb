# Changelog

All notable changes to the **dasVerb** project are documented here.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-19

### Features & Gamification
- **Monthly Leaderboard & Rankings**: Real-time monthly leaderboard with points scored from quiz completions, user ranks, and a historical Hall of Fame for past monthly champions.
- **Monthly Learning Recap**: Automated month-end summary modal celebrating personal accomplishments and leaderboard standing.
- **Streak Tracker Screen (`StreakScreen`)**: Dedicated screen featuring an interactive monthly activity calendar, streak freeze mechanics, and habit milestones.

### Practice & Quiz Modes
- **Hands-on Keyboard Input (Type-in Mode)**: Learners can now practice exact German verb spelling by typing answers directly, toggleable alongside multiple-choice tiles in app settings and quiz settings.

### Audio & Haptics Polish
- **System Haptic Feedback**: Distinct, crisp tactile feedback on button taps, option choices, and a dual head-shake vibration pattern on errors.
- **Interface Settings**: Brand-new settings section allowing users to toggle haptics and sound effects independently.
- **Conjugation Practice Audio Fix**: Eliminated erroneous TTS/audio download warnings in conjugation trainer mode.

### Localization & System
- Updated store metadata and changelogs across all 26 supported App Store and Google Play locales.
- General UI refinements, spacing adjustments in Conjugation Trainer, and performance optimizations.
