Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it[cite: 1]. This information may include[cite: 1]:

Data Deletion and Account Management

The Application does not maintain user accounts that require a password[cite: 1]. However, we associate purchase and diagnostic data with an internal identifier[cite: 1]. Users have the following options regarding their data[cite: 1]:

  • Local Data: All local learning progress and statistics can be deleted at any time through Settings > Reset Progress[cite: 1].
  • Remote Data Deletion: To request deletion of data associated with your internal identifier (such as purchase history), please use the Contact Us button in the Application's settings or email yarm.apps@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request"[cite: 1].
  • Subscriptions: Deleting data or uninstalling the app does not cancel active subscriptions[cite: 1]. Subscriptions must be managed through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store settings[cite: 1].
